Key Predictor Features

Our dataset contains a total of {{ total_features }} columns, with the following key attributes used for placement analysis:

CGPA (Continuous variable)

The cumulative grade point average of the student. High CGPA shows academic consistency.

Internships (Integer variable)

Number of relevant industry internships completed, indicating practical exposure.

Projects (Integer variable)

Number of capstone engineering projects built, indicating development skills.

HistoryOfBacklogs (Categorical variable)

Indicates whether the student has any outstanding academic backlogs.
